    What the AI is thinking ● offline

    Made for Penlock

    Split on paper. Read back in one photo.

    Penlock is a printable paper tool that splits your 12-word seed phrase into 3 shares. Keep them in different places. Any 2 bring your wallet back; 1 alone reveals nothing useful.

    Typing a share back in is slow and easy to get wrong. bitcoin-vision reads the whole share from a photo, every symbol, the share’s identity code and each row’s error check.

    Built for hardware wallets

    Small enough to live inside your wallet.

    The whole AI is about 25 MB and never needs the internet, so it can run inside a signing device. It already powers the Penlock app on Passport Prime, reading shares right on the hardware wallet.

    How it thinks.

    It doesn’t understand your wallet. It does one narrow job, turning handwriting into the right words, in four small steps.

    1 Find

    Straightens the photo and boxes every word on the page.

    absert absert

    2 Read

    Reads letter by letter. A sloppy “n” can look like an “r”.

    absent71%
    assert22%
    absurd4%

    3 Match

    Scores what it read against all 2048 official seed words.

    4 Check

    Tests all 12 words together with the backup’s built-in check.

    Unsure? It asks.

    Every word gets a confidence score. When two words look alike, or a read isn’t a real seed word, it stops and asks you.

    The built-in check catches most mistakes, but about 1 time in 16 a wrong word still slips through. Always compare with your paper.

    What it can’t do yet.

    It’s an early, open-source prototype. Here’s where it struggles.

    • English seed words only
    • Faint or messy writing can fool it
    • A passing check isn’t proof
    • Practice words only, never a real backup
